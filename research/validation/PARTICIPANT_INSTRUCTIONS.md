# Instructions for Participants

Thank you for helping validate the JCAB AI Assessment Framework!

## What is this?
This is a research prototype that uses Artificial Intelligence to conduct a conversational assessment on Java programming concepts. It analyzes your answers to provide highly personalized feedback.

## How to participate:
1. Open the application.
2. Read and agree to the participation notice.
3. Enter your Name or an Anonymous ID.
4. Click **Start Assessment**.
5. You will be asked a conceptual Java question. You can answer by **typing** or by **speaking** into your microphone.
6. Submit your answer. The AI will ask a follow-up question. Answer it as best as you can.
7. Click **Complete Concept**. The AI will analyze your conversation.
8. Review the score and the **Personalized Feedback**.
9. At the end, please fill out the short 1-5 rating survey to let us know how helpful the feedback was!

*Your responses are purely for academic research to improve AI-driven education.*
